Our Story

One hearth,
no shortcuts.

Kiln & Copper opened with a single rule: if it can't be cooked over live fire, it doesn't go on the menu. No gas, no induction, no exceptions. Chef Luca Marchetti built the kitchen around a custom-cut hearth so every table can watch the wood do the work.

Nine years later, that rule hasn't changed. We still split our own oak, dry-age our own beef, and close the kitchen the moment the coals go cold.

Luca Marchetti
Chef & Owner
